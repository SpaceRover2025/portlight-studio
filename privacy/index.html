Deckside privacy policy

Updated November 20, 2026

Portlight Studio develops Deckside. This policy covers the current playtest and this website. Your cabin uses a profile saved on your device. When you enter a public room, the game shares the information other players need to see, hear and play with you.

How to delete your data

Your profile and cabin

Deckside creates a random player ID on your device. It saves the name you choose, your outfit, unlocked cosmetics, coins and daily reward progress so they are there when you return. Your cabin settings, whiteboard drawings, movement and audio preferences, and lists of muted or blocked players also stay on your device. The current playtest uses local profiles. PlayFab account linking and real-money purchases are turned off.

What other passengers can see and hear

Deckside uses your headset and controller positions and rotations to move your avatar. In public rooms, Photon carries your player ID, display name, appearance, head and hand movements, gestures, movement mode and game actions. This lets other people see you point, pick up a basketball or draw a clue in Charades. They can see your name, avatar and the things you do in that room.

Your microphone starts muted. When you unmute, Photon Voice sends your microphone audio to the room so other players can hear you from your avatar’s direction. You can mute yourself again in the menu. Deckside sends text you submit to room chat to other players there. The chat window keeps the six most recent messages in memory and clears them when you disconnect.

On Windows, players can take a screenshot or record the game. Recordings include sound they can hear, including other players’ voices. The built-in capture tool saves the files on the recording player’s computer. That player can keep or share them.

Connections and safety

Photon runs the multiplayer and voice connections. Our build check runs on Cloudflare and checks the game platform, build version and, when provided, the player ID before allowing a connection. These providers receive connection information, such as an IP address, to route requests and operate their services.

If you file an in-game report, Deckside saves the player IDs of the people involved, the room, time, reason and any details you type. In this playtest the reporting service is switched off, so reports remain queued on the reporting device. Muting and blocking preferences are also saved locally.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. You can read more in GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection.

How to delete your data

To erase your Windows playtest profile, close Deckside, open File Explorer and enter %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Deckside\Deckside in the address bar. Delete the Player folder. This erases the local player ID, progress, drawings and queued reports. The next launch creates a new profile. You can delete your own screenshots and recordings separately in Videos → Deckside.

Wherever you live, you can ask Portlight Studio to delete data it holds about you, or ask a privacy question, by emailing privacy@portlight.studio. Deletion requests are free. Include your Deckside display name, the platform you played on, and enough detail to locate the data. A room name and approximate date can help. Please keep passwords and identity documents out of your message.

We use your email address and the information you send to locate the relevant records, verify the request where necessary and reply. If a service provider holds the relevant data on our behalf, we will ask it to delete the data too. If we cannot match a record or must retain it by law, we will explain why. We cannot remotely erase files saved on your device or recordings another player has kept.
