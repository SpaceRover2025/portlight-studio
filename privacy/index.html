Deckside privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026

Portlight Studio develops Deckside. This policy covers the standalone Meta Quest playtest and this website. Your cabin uses a profile saved on your headset. When you enter a public room, the game shares the information other players need to see, hear and play with you.

How to delete your data

Your profile and cabin

Your headset keeps a local profile for your cabin, display name, outfit, drawings, movement and audio preferences, muted and blocked players, and any local playtest progress. If Meta Cloud Backup includes Deckside, Meta may keep a backup of that app data.

When you launch the Quest alpha, Deckside checks your entitlement and asks Meta for your app-specific user ID and a one-time proof of identity. Our account service on Cloudflare verifies the proof with Meta. It stores a derived identifier and links your installation to a Microsoft PlayFab player account so you can recover the same account when you reinstall. PlayFab issues session credentials and maintains online account and inventory records. Deckside requests access to Meta's User Profile feature. The current Quest alpha does not display, store, or share your Meta Horizon username or profile photo. Other passengers see the display name you choose in Deckside.

What other passengers can see and hear

Deckside uses your headset and controller positions and rotations to move your avatar. In public rooms, Photon carries your player ID, display name, appearance, head and hand movements, gestures, movement mode and game actions. This lets other people see you point, pick up a basketball or draw a clue in Charades. They can see your name, avatar and the things you do in that room.

Your microphone starts muted. When you try to unmute, Deckside asks for microphone permission if you have not already granted it. With permission, the muted mic reminder measures the sound level on your headset so it can show a crossed-out microphone when you try to speak. The reminder is on by default. Its samples stay on your headset, are discarded after use and are never sent to other passengers. You can turn the reminder off under Settings → Player tools.

With your microphone unmuted in a connected voice room, Photon Voice sends your audio so other players can hear you from your avatar’s direction. You can mute yourself again in the menu. Deckside sends text you submit to room chat to other players there. The chat window keeps the six most recent messages in memory and clears them when you disconnect.

Connections and safety

Photon runs multiplayer and voice. Our service on Cloudflare checks the platform and build version and gives a signed-in Quest player a short-lived credential for Photon. These providers receive connection information, such as an IP address, to route requests and operate their services.

If you file an in-game report, Deckside queues it on your device. When delivery succeeds, our account service stores the player IDs involved, room, time, reason and details you submitted, and records the case privately on the reported player's PlayFab account for moderation. Muting and blocking preferences stay on your headset.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. You can read more in GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection.

How to delete your data

Close Deckside, open Storage in your headset settings, find Deckside and choose the option to clear its app data. This removes the local player ID, progress, drawings and queued reports from that headset. If you used more than one headset, repeat these steps on each.

If Meta Cloud Backup saved a copy, find Deckside in your Meta account’s Cloud Backups and delete each backup you want to remove. A backup left there can restore old data when you reinstall the game. Meta’s backup deletion instructions

Clearing your headset leaves your online account in place. To request deletion of your PlayFab account and its link to Deckside, contact Portlight Studio using the address below.

Wherever you live, you can ask Portlight Studio to delete data it holds about you, or ask a privacy question, by emailing privacy@portlight.studio. Deletion requests are free. Include your Deckside display name and enough detail to locate the data. A room name and approximate date can help. Please keep passwords and identity documents out of your message.

We use your email address and the information you send to locate the relevant records, verify the request where necessary and reply. If a service provider holds the relevant data on our behalf, we will ask it to delete the data too. If we cannot match a record or must retain it by law, we will explain why. Only you can clear the local app data on your headset.
